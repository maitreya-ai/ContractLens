# VaultToken Whitepaper

VaultToken (VLT) is a community token for the Vault ecosystem. This document is a fictional
example bundled with ContractLens to show how claims in documentation can be checked against code.

## Vision

VaultToken rewards long-term holders and funds ecosystem development through a small transfer tax.
We believe in fair launches, transparent tokenomics and giving control to the community.

## Tokenomics

### Supply

The total supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 VLT. No new tokens can ever be minted after launch,
so holders never face dilution.

### Distribution

- 60% liquidity pool
- 20% community rewards
- 10% team, vested over 24 months
- 10% marketing and partnerships

### Transfer tax

A tax of 3% applies to buys and 5% to sells. The tax can never exceed 5% and funds the
marketing wallet.

## Security

### Ownership

Ownership of the contract will be renounced within one week of launch. After that no one,
including the team, can change fees or pause trading.

### Immutability

The VaultToken contract is immutable. Its logic cannot be upgraded or replaced.

### Liquidity

Liquidity is locked for 12 months using a third-party locker.

### Wallet limits

To prevent whales from accumulating, no wallet may hold more than 2% of the supply.

## Roadmap

1. Fair launch and liquidity lock
2. Ownership renounced
3. Staking vaults
4. Cross-chain bridge
